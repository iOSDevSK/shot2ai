Other destinations

Send a screenshot into a web chat instead. The extension pastes it into the chat's message box and you press Enter there, unless you turn on Send automatically.

A web chat is a website. A screenshot you send there goes to that site and is handled under its terms, not only to this Mac. Chrome asks you to allow each site once. Send automatically (off by default) presses the chat's send button after pasting, so the message goes without you reviewing it; it is never used for html2wp. Send to all selected sends to every ticked site.

Auto-submit presses the send button on a third-party website for you. Some services restrict automated use in their terms; you are responsible for using it within them.

Full-page capture

Scrolls the page and joins the screens into one image. Pages that keep loading more (infinite feeds) stop at this height, or after 30 screens.

Keyboard shortcuts

Chrome manages the keys; if another extension already uses one, Chrome leaves it unset.

Floating toolbar

A small Shot2AI bar on every page with Area, Visible, Region and your destination. Drag it by its grip, collapse it to a dot, or hide it on a site from its ⋯ menu.

To appear on every page, the toolbar needs access to all sites; Chrome asks you when you switch it on. Shot2AI reads nothing on those pages: it only draws the toolbar and captures when you click it. Switching it off gives up the all-site access. It appears on pages you open or reload after switching it on.

Privacy

What Shot2AI handles, and where it goes. Full privacy policy

  • Captured: screenshots of the page you are on, only when you ask; the message you type; text you selected for "Send selection"; the page's address and title, to label the capture.
  • Sent: only where you send it. html2wp is the app on this computer (127.0.0.1); a web chat is the site you chose, under its own terms. There is no Shot2AI server.
  • Stored: settings, prompts and destinations in Chrome's extension storage; captures in the extension's IndexedDB (deleted after a day); all on this device.
  • Never: no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no sale of data.

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
