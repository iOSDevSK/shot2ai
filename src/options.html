Other destinations

Send a screenshot into a web chat instead. With Send automatically (on for ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity) the extension sends it in the chat's own tab, which stays in the background, and shows the answer in the card on your page. Turn it off to have the chat's tab come to the front with the screenshot pasted, so you press Enter there.

A web chat is a website. A screenshot you send there goes to that site and is handled under its terms, not only to this Mac. Chrome asks you to allow each site once. Send automatically presses the chat's send button once the screenshot has uploaded, so the message goes without you reviewing it. It is on for ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity and off for chats you add; it is never used for html2wp. For those three, Shot2AI then reads the answer (and Perplexity's sources) on that chat's page and shows it in the card; it reads nothing else there. Shot2AI uses the chat's tab you keep open, signed in, so each screenshot continues that conversation; if you close it, it opens again on your last conversation. Send to all selected sends to every ticked site.

Auto-submit presses the send button on a third-party website for you. Some services restrict automated use in their terms; you are responsible for using it within them.

Prompts

Saved messages to send with a screenshot. Pick one in the card or the editor, or right-click → Shot2AI → Send with prompt.

The first prompt is the default. Move a prompt to the top to change it. An empty message uses the default prompt when sent.

Keyboard shortcuts

Chrome manages the keys; if another extension already uses one, Chrome leaves it unset.

Floating toolbar

A small Shot2AI bar on every page with Area, Visible, Region and your destination. Drag it by its grip, collapse it to a dot, or hide it on a site from its ⋯ menu.

To appear on every page, the toolbar needs access to all sites; Chrome asks you when you switch it on. Shot2AI reads nothing on those pages: it only draws the toolbar and captures when you click it. Switching it off gives up the all-site access unless Website integrations still uses it. It appears on pages you open or reload after switching it on.

Privacy

What Shot2AI handles, and where it goes. Full privacy policy

  • Captured: screenshots of the page you are on, only when you ask; the message you type; text you explicitly selected for sending, with your prompt and no image when you choose "Send selected text"; the page's address and title, to label the capture.
  • Sent: only where you send it. html2wp is the app on this computer (127.0.0.1); a web chat is the site you chose, under its own terms. Choosing WhatsApp, Facebook or X in Share uploads a public copy to the Shot2AI sharing server for 30 days.
  • Read back: after Shot2AI sends a screenshot or selected text to ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity for you, it reads that chat's answer on the chat's page and shows it in the card on your page. It keeps the answer with the capture until you close the card (a day at most) and uploads it only when you choose social sharing.
  • Stored: settings, prompts and destinations in Chrome's extension storage, with the model you chose for each known chat and the model names read from its picker, and the address of your last conversation with it (so a closed chat tab opens again there); captures and their answers in the extension's IndexedDB (deleted after a day); on this device, except public copies you explicitly share.
  • Never: no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no sale of data.

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Manage or delete public shared links

This deletes every capture, your settings, prompts, destinations, saved regions and the html2wp pairing, and turns the floating toolbar off. Public shared links stay available until they expire; delete them above first if needed. Clearing also removes your ability to delete them early. It cannot be undone.
